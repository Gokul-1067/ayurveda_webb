# Dr. Varsha’s Ayurvedic Treatment Center
## Minimal Visual Website Outline / Client Prototype

A clean, calm, minimal website outline created strictly as a **visual layout prototype** to show a potential client what their future website structure and design direction will look like.

---

### 🌿 Purpose & Scope

- **This is NOT a finished website.**
- **This is NOT content-filled.**
- **This is NOT a functional back-end system.**
- **Zero images used:** Neutral `[ IMAGE PLACEHOLDER ]` boxes are used everywhere actual photography or assets will eventually be placed.
- **Short placeholder text only:** Shows exactly where client-provided information, treatment details, doctor bio, FAQs, and testimonials will go without inventing medical claims or realistic stories.

---

### 🎨 Visual & Theme Direction

- **Background:** Beige / warm cream (`#F8F5EE`, `#F1ECE1`)
- **Headings & Hierarchy:** Dark muted green (`#1C2F22`)
- **Accents:** Muted sage green (`#6B7D68`) & light muted green (`#E2EBE0`)
- **Layout:** High whitespace, clean rectangular cards, flat subtle borders, zero heavy shadows, zero flashy effects or animations.

---

### 📄 Pages Included

1. **HOME (`index.html`)**
   - Clean Hero: Business name, short intro placeholder, `VIEW TREATMENTS` and `CONTACT US` buttons, hero image placeholder.
   - Section 1: About / Introduction (`[ IMAGE PLACEHOLDER ]` + “About the center goes here.”)
   - Section 2: Treatment Plans (4 clean rectangular cards with image placeholders)
   - Section 3: Why Choose Us (3 simple concept feature cards)
   - Section 4: Testimonials (3 simple cards: “Client testimonial goes here.” — Client Name)
   - Section 5: FAQ (Clean single-expand accordion: Question goes here? / Answer placeholder)
   - Section 6: Contact (Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Location placeholders + simple inquiry form)
2. **ABOUT (`about.html`)**
   - Structural outline for center background, treatment philosophy, and Dr. Varsha's profile with image placeholders.
3. **TREATMENTS (`treatments.html`)**
   - Rectangular treatment grid layout showing where service photos, titles, and descriptions will appear.
4. **TESTIMONIALS (`testimonials.html`)**
   - Feedback section with 3 clean testimonial cards.
5. **FAQ (`faq.html`)**
   - Simple accordion structure demonstrating patient inquiry organization.
6. **CONTACT (`contact.html`)**
   - Communication channels (Phone, WhatsApp, Email, Location) and inquiry form layout.

---
