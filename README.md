# Dr. Varsha’s Ayurvedic Treatment Center & Sanctuary
### Visual Concept Prototype & Presentation Website

A bespoke, calm, minimalistic, and premium website prototype created from scratch for **Dr. Varsha’s Ayurvedic Treatment Center**.

---

## 🌿 Concept & Design Direction

- **Atmosphere & Tone:** Minimalistic, elegant, calm, natural, and trustworthy. Built to convey high clinical authority blended with serene hospitality (reminiscent of high-end sanctuaries like Ananda in the Himalayas, Soukyam, and CGH Earth).
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Base Backgrounds:** Warm beige / soothing cream (`#FAF7F2`, `#F3EFE8`, `#EDE7DC`).
  - **Botanical Tones:** Muted sage green (`#8A9E86`, `#687D64`, `#DFE8DD`) for subtle badges, borders, and accents.
  - **Primary & Headings:** Deep muted forest green (`#18281E`, `#22382B`) providing clear visual contrast and editorial poise.
  - **Earth Accents:** Subtle hints of warm terracotta, raw clay & sandalwood (`#B27852`, `#F6EDE6`).
  - **Zero High-Saturation Neons:** Free of cheap cliparts or garish spa cliches.
- **Typography:**
  - Headings & Editorial Titles: *Cormorant Garamond* (Google Fonts)
  - Body Copy & Micro-copy: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Google Fonts)

## ✨ Key Interactive Features Built into the Demo

1. **Top Announcement Bar:** Subtle seasonal notification banner for *Ritu Sandhi* retreat registrations with direct modal trigger and dismiss option.
2. **Sticky Glassmorphic Header:** Brand monogram, full navigation, quick telephone dialer, and "Book Consultation" CTA with mobile-friendly drawer.
3. **Hero Section:** High-impact editorial headline, clinical trust credentials (22+ years heritage, 100% classical formulas), and floating pulse diagnosis badges.
4. **Pillars of Classical Care:** Four foundational philosophy cards:
   - *Nadi Pariksha* (Eight-fold Pulse Diagnostics)
   - *Shodhana Chikitsa* (Classical Panchakarma Detoxification)
   - *Dravyaguna Alchemy* (Purity-tested Classical Medicines)
   - *Dinacharya & Ahara* (Circadian Lifestyle Architecture)
5. **Therapies & Treatments Showcase:**
   - Filterable tabs: *All Therapies*, *Panchakarma & Detox*, *Stress & Sleep*, *Musculoskeletal & Pain*, *Rejuvenation & Skin*.
   - Six signature treatments (*Shirodhara*, *Abhyanga & Swedana*, *Panchakarma Retreat Protocol*, *Kati & Janu Basti*, *Udvartana Scrub*, and *Soundarya Mukha Lepam*).
   - Interactive "View Full Protocol" button opening detailed medical information with classical formulation lists and clinical indications.
6. **Senior Physician Profile (Dr. Varsha):**
   - Portrait, medical credentials (Gold Medalist BAMS, MD Kayachikitsa & Panchakarma, All India Ayurvedic Congress).
   - Philosophy quote, clinical biography, and clinical specialty tags.
7. **Interactive Prakriti & Dosha Self-Assessment Tool:**
   - 4-step diagnostic questionnaire measuring physical energy, digestion (*Agni*), sleep patterns, and constitutional frame.
   - Real-time animated score bars showing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha percentages.
   - Tailored clinical recommendations and pre-filled consultation booking.
8. **The 4-Stage Patient Healing Journey:**
   - *Roga & Rogi Pariksha* (Diagnostic evaluation)
   - *Purva Karma* (Preparatory oleation)
   - *Pradhana Karma* (Panchakarma therapies)
   - *Paschat Karma & Rasayana* (Longevity and lifestyle sustenance)
9. **The Sanctuary Environment Gallery:**
   - Large photo showcase of the quiet Shirodhara chambers, botanical dispensary, and meditative reflection courtyards.
10. **Patient Case Testimonials:**
    - Real-world clinical recoveries (Chronic Migraine & Insomnia, Metabolic Fatigue & IBS, Lumbar Spondylosis).
    - Google Rating badge (4.9 / 5.0 with 340+ reviews).
11. **Vedic Wellness Journal (Blog & Insights):**
    - 3 clinical essays by Dr. Varsha (*Circadian Agni*, *Dinacharya Morning Habits*, and *The Neuroscience of Shirodhara*).
    - Interactive "Read Full Essay" reader modal.
12. **Frequently Asked Questions Accordion:**
    - Clean single-expand accordion answering essential clinical and pre-visit questions.
13. **Sanctuary Location & Hours:**
    - Indiranagar enclave address, visiting hours, contact details, and a minimalist architectural map card.
14. **Interactive Consultation Booking Flow (Prototype):**
    - Select treatment, date, and preferred morning/afternoon/evening time slots.
    - Demonstrates instant confirmation screen with instructions and appointment summary.
15. **Toast Notification Feedback:**
    - Elegant feedback toasts for WhatsApp concierge and newsletter subscription.

## 🚀 How to View & Present to the Client

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Simply double-click `index.html` or open it directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox.

### Option 2: Local Web Server (via Python)
Run this command from this folder in PowerShell:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit:
[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)
